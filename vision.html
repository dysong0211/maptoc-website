For governments and funders

See the program while there is still time to improve it.

Set a shared minimum for recording significant decisions, changes and outcome evidence. Keep what is known—and what remains uncertain—available for review throughout delivery.

  • Review material exceptions while decisions can still change.
  • Read spending alongside the events and approvals behind it.
  • Identify recurring risks and missing evidence across programs.
  • Keep decisions available through staff handovers and closure.
  • Reuse the same reviewed record for oversight and reporting.
01The opportunity

Make oversight useful during delivery.

Governments and funders oversee many programs through changing teams and grant cycles. A common evidence record can help them spot exceptions, ask better questions and understand the reasons behind reported progress before a program closes.

FOR FUNDERS AND GOVERNMENTS

White Paper · How capture and review work ↗
For governments and funders

See the program while there is still time to improve it.

A funded program should leave behind an honest, source-linked account of what happened, what changed, what was learned and what remains uncertain—while there is still time to act.

  • Material exceptions become visible earlier.
  • Spending can be read with delivery context.
  • Recurring risks and documentation gaps can be acted on before closure.
  • Program managers do not have to reconstruct history from staff turnover and scattered files.
  • Funders can set a minimum evidence requirement without forcing a new report template every quarter.
01The opportunity

Why institutions make the record standard.

Governments and foundations make decisions across changing teams, partners and grant cycles. The opportunity is to make the evidence accumulated in one program useful to the people deciding what comes next.

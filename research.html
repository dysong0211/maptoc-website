FROM THE LITERATURE

Three findings worth investigating.

Different sources examine different problems. These findings should not be combined into a single estimate of reporting failure.

SYSTEMATIC REVIEW · 202059%

CFIR applied at endline

Of 34 studies in 21 low- and middle-income countries, 59% used the framework at study endline, mainly for analysis or interpretation.

What does this tell us?

It describes the timing of one framework in the reviewed studies, not the share of all programs evaluated too late.

UN INSPECTION · 2025Cost gap

Additional oversight has a workload

The UN JIU identifies costs from additional donor requests and limited information on those costs.

What should we watch for?

Repeated requests can create burden, but the review does not quantify a universal percentage of wasted spending.

METHODS · 2019Context

Adaptation changes what is evaluated

FRAME documents what changed, when, who decided and why. These details help describe the intervention actually delivered.

What remains uncertain?

Documenting a change does not prove that it improved outcomes. Its effects still need assessment.

Editorial approach

This collection offers selective evidence briefs and methodological analysis. It is not a peer-reviewed journal or a systematic review. Original study authors are credited in the references; the editorial synthesis is separate from their findings. Preliminary internal audit figures are identified separately from published research.

White Paper / Building MapToc

Institutional case

Why institutions may make a living evidence record standard across funded programs.

Explore the vision →

MapToc's proposed value is continuous institutional visibility from award to closure. It gives funders, governments and organizations one reviewed record connecting resources, decisions, delivery and outcomes, while reducing the manual reconstruction required for each reporting or review cycle.

The institutional value proposition

MapToc targets governments, foundations, development funders and organizations that need a continuous view of implementation and outcomes across funded programs. One evolving evidence record supports management, grant oversight, reporting, evaluation preparation and audit readiness.

The product hypothesis is that connecting decisions, adaptations, risks, expenditure context and outcome evidence over time creates stronger institutional intelligence than reports assembled after the fact. Source links and AI summaries alone are not a moat. Advantage must be demonstrated through lower reconstruction effort, reliable review history, useful outcome relationships and integration with institutional workflows.

MapToc is proposed as a SaaS subscription for programs and portfolios. A paid institutional pilot should begin with a bounded workflow and named decision point. Subscription scope, licensing, onboarding and pricing will be tested in pilots. Making the record a funding requirement is the adoption ambition after value is established; no current mandate, partnership or procurement commitment is claimed.

Portfolio intelligence depends on comparable definitions, sufficient evidence and permissioned access. It should reveal context and evidence limitations, not rank unlike programs as if their outcomes were interchangeable.

Why institutions would adopt MapToc

  • Automated accountability: structure evidence as work happens and keep claims connected to sources.
  • Frictionless oversight: review current program records without repeatedly commissioning a new information request.
  • Live program integrity: keep missing information, contradictions, decisions and unexplained changes visible while action remains possible.
  • Real-time visibility: see funding context, delivery and outcome evidence across authorized programs from one institutional view.

These phrases describe intended product value. Pilots must test the actual level of automation, reporting effort saved, timeliness and decision usefulness.

What partners can help establish

Program partners can provide a realistic workflow, an authorized source collection and staff feedback. M&E specialists can help define evidence quality and appropriate review. Evaluators can test whether the resulting record improves their starting point. These are possible partnership roles, not a list of confirmed partners.

What funders and investors should look for

The early milestones are a functioning review process, repeat use by a program team and a credible account of total effort and usefulness. Adoption matters as much as extraction accuracy. A technically impressive prototype has limited value if staff cannot keep the record current or trust its source links.

Commercial questions remain open

Pricing, procurement requirements, support needs and the eventual revenue model are still to be tested. No market-size estimate, committed investment or revenue claim is made here. The first value case should be grounded in observed workflows and a clear comparison with the organization's existing approach.

Dependencies to examine

The proposition depends on access to useful source material, human review capacity, permission controls and the ability to integrate with organizational practice. It also depends on whether a program actually uses the record for decisions and reporting.

The next conversation

The immediate invitation is to discuss a bounded pilot, research collaboration, product feedback or development support. A useful first conversation identifies the program, the reporting problem, who would use the record and how both sides would assess whether it helped.
